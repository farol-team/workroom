WorkRoom for Meetings

Every meeting leaves the room smarter than it found it.

Most organizational knowledge is created out loud — in calls, standups, reviews — and most of it evaporates within a day. WorkRoom gives every participant an AI agent, and gives the team a channel where every meeting's outcome becomes something the room keeps.

The expensive amnesia of meetings

An hour of five people's time produces ten minutes of value — if anyone remembers it.

Decisions without a home

"So we're agreed?" — yes, and by next week two people remember two different versions, and the reasoning exists nowhere at all.

Action items decay

Commitments made out loud die quietly: nobody's agent was there to keep them, so they resurface only when they're already late.

Absence is permanent

Miss the meeting and the nuance is gone — a summary doc captures the agenda, not the concern in someone's voice when the deadline was mentioned.

How it works

Your agent was at every previous meeting — even the ones you missed.

Drop the notes or transcript in the channel

Your agent is rehydrated first: it knows every prior call's conclusions and open commitments — before it reads a line of today's notes.

The agent distils outcomes, not minutes

Decisions with their reasoning, action items with owners, the concern behind the offhand remark — summarized with the full history of the relationship in view.

The next meeting starts where this one ended

Commitments are tracked, decisions have provenance, and anyone who missed the call catches up by asking their agent — not by reading a transcript.
