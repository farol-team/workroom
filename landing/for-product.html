WorkRoom for Product

Decisions stay decided.

Product work is a chain of judgment calls: what to build, what to descope, what the research actually said. WorkRoom gives every PM an AI agent — and gives the team a shared memory of decisions with their reasoning, so the same question never has to be settled twice.

The expensive amnesia in product

The roadmap moves forward. The reasoning behind it keeps getting lost.

"Why did we decide that?"

The decision is in a doc somewhere. The reasoning — the data, the trade-off, the dissent that was overruled — lived in a discussion nobody can reconstruct a quarter later.

Descoped work comes back unexamined

A feature killed for solid reasons returns as a fresh idea from someone new — and gets seriously considered again, because the reasons died with the meeting.

Research insights evaporate

Forty user interviews produced five real insights. Two quarters later the team is commissioning research to rediscover three of them.
