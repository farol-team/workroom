WorkRoom for Support

Every solved case makes the next one faster.

Support speed is really memory speed: has anyone seen this before, and what fixed it? WorkRoom gives every agent-handling teammate their own AI agent — and gives the team a shared memory of issues and resolutions, so nothing gets diagnosed twice.

The expensive amnesia in support

Ticket systems track cases. The knowledge that resolves them lives elsewhere — or nowhere.

Known issues get re-diagnosed

The error with the five-minute fix gets investigated for two hours by the next person, because the fix lives in a closed ticket nobody searches — or a colleague's head.

Customer context doesn't transfer

The account with the unusual SSO setup, the one that breaks on every cert rotation — re-surprised by it every time, because it's nobody's job to write it down.

Escalations lose the thread

By the time a case reaches engineering, the diagnosis work done at tier one has to be redone — the path was walked, but the trail wasn't kept.
